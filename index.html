Why fibreglass?

Lighter than steel.
Outlasts it too.

Fibreglass is lightweight, economical, corrosion-resistant, flexible and extremely strong. In chemical service it consistently outperforms steel and aluminium.

Light Weight

As a composite, FRP offers a strength-to-weight ratio far higher than metals, so it is easier to transport, lift and install.

Corrosion Resistant

Resin systems are formulated for the specific chemical, so FRP thrives in corrosive atmospheres where steel fails.

Temperature Resistant

Suitable for hot or cold extremes and every kind of weather, indoors or outdoors.

Economical

High strength and corrosion resistance give an average service life of over 35 years with minimal upkeep.

Design Freedom

Any shape, size or finish, engineered to the relevant international standards.

Easily Repaired

Repairs, modifications and additional fittings can be carried out on site.

About Laybond

From one technician to a full-scale FRP works

Laybond began in 1994 in a rented shed in Ambattur, Chennai, with one FRP technician and a commitment to quality.

Today we operate from an 8,000 sq ft manufacturing facility on more than an acre at SIPCOT Industrial Complex, Pudukkottai, allotted by the Government of Tamil Nadu. Our team of 50+ people serves industries across India, and we train local youth in FRP fabrication skills.

  • ISO 9001 certified quality system
  • Designed to BS 4994, ASTM D-3299 / D-4097 & ASME RTP-1
  • Factory-built tanks up to 50 m³; larger tanks built on site
  • Installation, commissioning, maintenance & emergency service
