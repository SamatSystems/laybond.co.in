About Laybond

Three decades of composite engineering: designing, fabricating and installing corrosion-resistant equipment for India's process industries.

Who we are

A total composite engineering company

Laybond designs, fabricates and supplies FRP and dual-laminate thermoplastic products, and takes on complete turnkey projects.

We manufacture contact-moulded tanks from 1 m³ to 300 m³ for chemical processing, food processing, iron & steel, wastewater, metal finishing, bulk storage, pharmaceutical and other applications. Tanks up to 50 m³ are built in our factory; larger tanks are fabricated on site.

We build cone-bottom, dish-bottom, flat-bottom, slope-bottom, horizontal and vertical tanks, designed to BS 4994-1987, ASTM D-3299 and D-4097, with ASME RTP-1 design-by-rules available on request.

How it happened

From a single technician in a rented shed to a government-allotted manufacturing campus.

Where it started

Victor, our founder and CEO, begins his career at an FRP tank manufacturer in Chennai.

1994

Laybond is born

Victor starts Laybond Pipings & Equipments with a single FRP technician, building tanks in a rented shed in Ambattur Industrial Area, Chennai.

2003

First dedicated facility

We move to a new 3,200 sq ft facility in Ambattur SIDCO Industrial Estate, Chennai.

2009

SIPCOT Pudukkottai

We relocate to a site at SIPCOT Industrial Complex, Pudukkottai, allotted by the Government of Tamil Nadu: 8,000 sq ft of manufacturing space on more than an acre.

Today

50+ people, pan-India projects

A growing team serving power, process and manufacturing industries, and training local youth in FRP skills.
