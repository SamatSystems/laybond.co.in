Construction

Laminate construction

Every Laybond tank is built on a male mould for a smooth, resin-rich internal surface, the first line of defence against chemical attack.

The corrosion barrier, made up of the inner surface and interior layer, has a glass content of 27% ± 5% by weight. A structural layer behind it carries the mechanical loads, finished with a protective outer surface.

Engineering details

Design features

Tank bottoms

Flat and sloped bottoms use a seamless, one-piece bottom-to-wall construction with a generous knuckle radius at the corner. Cone and dished bottoms are one-piece and seamless, with a steel support ring overlaid with fibreglass.

Tops

Closed tops come with a 20″ or 24″ loose-cover manhole with grab handle. Open tops have a 90° flanged rim (one-, two- or three-piece cover) or a Type II rim.

Nozzles

Flanged nozzles are hand lay-up construction, with conical and plate gussets available for added strength.

Insulation & heating

Insulated tanks have a reinforced FRP outer shell. A replaceable heat panel is offered as an alternative to wire heating systems.
