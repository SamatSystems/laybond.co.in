Overview

FRP storage tanks built for aggressive chemicals

Laybond FRP tanks safely store bleach, sodium hydroxide, sulphuric acid, hydrochloric acid and many other corrosive and caustic chemicals across a wide range of industries.

Tanks are engineered to BS 4994-1987. Most are cylindrical, but rectangular and custom shapes are available.

Tank types

Choose the bottom and top configuration that suits your process, drainage and site constraints.

Our most common design, built to ASTM D-3299 or D-4097. Requires a flat, fully supporting foundation.

Top options

  • Closed dome top: Our most requested top. ASME flanged-and-dished design with a standard top manhole and loose cover.
  • Closed flat top: Fixed flat surface where headspace is limited, agitator rails are required, or a walking surface is needed.
  • Open top, 90° flanged rim: For a flat cover, agitator rails or full access to the tank.
  • Open top, Type II rim: The most economical option when no cover or rails are needed.

Materials

Resin systems & dual laminate

We build to customer specification using polyester, bisphenol and vinyl ester thermoset resins. Where FRP alone will not work, a dual-laminate construction usually will.

What is dual laminate?

A hybrid construction: a strong fibreglass composite structure on the outside and a specialised thermoplastic liner on the inside. The liner is chosen to match the specific chemistry for maximum service life.

  • Mechanical strength of an FRP composite exterior
  • Corrosion-resistant thermoplastic interior for hazardous chemistry
  • High-temperature and abrasion resistance
  • Resists leaching agents and chemical attack
  • Suitable for high-purity processes

Liner options: PVC · CPVC · PP · HDPE and others, selected per chemical. See our thermoplastic corrosion chart.
