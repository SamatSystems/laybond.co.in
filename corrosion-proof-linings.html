Overview

Protect and restore steel & concrete

Corrosion from acids, caustics and aggressive chemicals costs industry heavily every year in repairs, replacement and downtime. Much of it is avoidable with the right lining, correctly applied.

Laybond is an experienced supplier and applicator of high-performance coatings and lining systems for tanks, vessels, silos, pipes and other steel and concrete structures. Long-standing relationships with leading corrosion-control manufacturers let us recommend the right system, even for severe process or storage duty.

On site, our crews work fast and as a team, in full cooperation with your production department and in line with your site rules, health & safety regulations and industry best practice.

Thick-film FRP lining

The smarter alternative to new tank bottoms

A thick-film FRP lining is a proven alternative to replacing corroded steel tank bottoms, mitigating internal and even external corrosion.

  • Vinyl ester resin systems resist many corrosive cargoes at elevated temperatures
  • Costs less than replacing existing steel tank bottoms
  • Faster installation and minimal downtime; tanks return to service sooner
  • Many installations with a 35+ year service history

Materials

Resin systems & dual laminate

We build to customer specification using polyester, bisphenol and vinyl ester thermoset resins. Where FRP alone will not work, a dual-laminate construction usually will.

What is dual laminate?

A hybrid construction: a strong fibreglass composite structure on the outside and a specialised thermoplastic liner on the inside. The liner is chosen to match the specific chemistry for maximum service life.

  • Mechanical strength of an FRP composite exterior
  • Corrosion-resistant thermoplastic interior for hazardous chemistry
  • High-temperature and abrasion resistance
  • Resists leaching agents and chemical attack
  • Suitable for high-purity processes

Liner options: PVC · CPVC · PP · HDPE and others, selected per chemical. See our thermoplastic corrosion chart.
